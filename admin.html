Stock manager

This is how you would run the forecourt yourself — no developer, no code. Flick the switch to stamp a car SOLD, or remove it from the site completely. Then open the Cars for Sale page and you will see the change straight away.

Try it: toggle a car to SOLD below, then click Cars for Sale. The card greys out with a SOLD stamp, drops off the home page and stops counting towards "available now". Everything you change here is remembered.
